      How to use Hands on Deck

      1. Brief. Say who the deck is for, the outcome you need and the key information. Try an example if you are unsure.
      2. Edit. Click any text on the slide. Use Add element for a chart, image, table or callout. Select a slide in the left rail to move, duplicate or delete it.
      3. Review. Follow the suggested checks, then use Present to check the whole story. Use the arrow buttons or ← →; press Esc to leave presentation mode.
      4. Save & share. An editable copy keeps your deck and the editor together. A presentation copy is for viewing. Download again after making changes.

      Your notes and images are not uploaded. Supercharged downloads a local model only when you choose to enable it. Browser recovery is a convenience; keep an editable download as your backup.
