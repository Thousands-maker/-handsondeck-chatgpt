        How to use Hands on Deck

        1. Brief. Say who the deck is for, the outcome you need and the key information. Try an example if you are unsure.
        2. Refine. Click any text on the slide. Switch to Outline to revise the argument and reorder slides. Use Source brief to compare the wording with your notes.
        3. Check. Follow the suggested checks, then use Present to check the whole story. Use the arrow buttons or ← →; press Esc to leave presentation mode.
        4. Download. An editable copy keeps your deck and the editor together. A presentation copy is for viewing. Download again after making changes.

        Your notes and images are not uploaded. Supercharged downloads a local model only when you choose to enable it. Browser recovery is a convenience; keep an editable download as your backup.
